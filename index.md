# Privacy Policy for Budgets

**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 13, 2026

Budgets is designed to help you plan and manage everyday spending while collecting as little personal information as reasonably necessary.

You can use Budgets without creating an account. Cloud Sync is optional, and anonymous usage analytics are disabled unless you explicitly choose to share them.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Budgets stores, what information may be transmitted when you use Cloud Sync or optional analytics, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

---

## 1. Financial Data

Budgets allows you to create and manage financial information such as:

- Budgets
- Plans and Plan Budgets
- Expenses
- Transfers
- Budget allocations
- Budget Period settings and history
- rollover information
- Recurring financial planning information
- notes or descriptions you choose to enter
- expense attachments you choose to add

Financial data is used only to provide Budgets functionality. It is not used for advertising or behavioral tracking.

### Recurring Financial Planning Data

If you use **Recurring**, Budgets may store information you provide about recurring income and fixed costs.

This may include:

- Income names or descriptions
- Income amounts
- Income frequency or cadence
- Fixed Cost names or descriptions
- Fixed Cost amounts
- Fixed Cost frequency or cadence
- Fixed Cost category or type
- amounts you choose to set aside or save

Examples may include salary or pension income, housing or utility costs, transportation costs, insurance, debt payments, subscriptions or memberships, family-care expenses, and money you choose to set aside or save.

Recurring information is treated as financial data in the same way as your Budgets, Plans, Expenses, and Transfers.

Recurring financial data is **not included in anonymous usage analytics**.

### Local-Only Use

You can use Budgets without signing in.

When you use Budgets without signing in, your financial data is stored locally on your device.

Budgets does not require you to:

- connect a bank account
- provide banking credentials
- connect a credit card
- automatically import transactions from a financial institution

If you do not use Cloud Sync, Budgets does not transmit your locally stored financial records to the Budgets synchronization service.

### Cloud Sync

If you choose to sign in and use **Cloud Sync**, Budgets transmits the financial information necessary to synchronize your data between authorized devices and restore synchronized data to another or reinstalled device.

Cloud synchronization may include:

- Budgets
- Plans and Plan Budgets
- Expenses
- Transfers
- Budget allocations
- Budget Period and rollover information
- recurring Income
- recurring Fixed Costs
- Savings or Set Aside records
- recurring frequencies or cadences
- Fixed Cost categories
- financial history required for accurate calculations
- notes or descriptions you enter
- expense attachment metadata
- expense attachment files

Cloud data is associated with your Budgets account so that it can be securely synchronized across authorized devices.

Financial information synchronized through Budgets is not used for advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-app tracking.

---

## 2. Sign in with Apple

Budgets may offer **Sign in with Apple** for users who want Cloud Sync.

Authentication information is used to identify your Budgets cloud account and authorize access to synchronized financial data.

Budgets does not use your Apple identity for advertising or tracking.

If Apple provides a private relay email address through Sign in with Apple, Budgets may receive that address as part of the authentication process where necessary to operate your account.

Authentication information is kept separate from Budgets' anonymous usage analytics.

---

## 3. Anonymous Usage Analytics

Budgets includes optional anonymous usage analytics to help improve the app.

**Analytics are disabled until you explicitly choose to share them.**

You may choose **Share Anonymous Analytics** when asked, or enable or disable analytics later in Budgets Settings.

Declining analytics does not limit Budgets functionality.

### What Anonymous Analytics May Include

If you opt in, Budgets may collect information such as:

- app opens and foreground activity
- whether a Budget was created
- whether an Expense was created
- whether a Plan was created
- whether a Plan Budget was created
- whether Spending Pace was viewed
- whether pull-to-refresh was used
- whether sign-in or sign-out occurred
- whether synchronization succeeded or failed
- general, non-sensitive synchronization failure categories
- Budgets app version and build number
- whether the app was signed in at the time of an event
- a randomly generated installation identifier
- a randomly generated session identifier

Budgets does not include your financial record contents in anonymous analytics.

### Anonymous Installation Identifier

Budgets generates a random identifier for the app installation when anonymous analytics are enabled.

This identifier:

- is not your Apple ID
- is not your Budgets account ID
- is not your email address
- is not an advertising identifier
- is not based on your device serial number
- is not based on a hardware identifier
- is not linked by Budgets to your authenticated account

Signed-in and signed-out users use the same anonymous analytics system.

Budgets does not maintain a table or other mapping that connects the anonymous installation identifier to your Budgets account.

### What Anonymous Analytics Do Not Include

Anonymous analytics do **not** include:

- financial amounts
- Budget names
- Plan names
- Plan Budget names
- Recurring Income names
- Recurring Income amounts
- Fixed Cost names
- Fixed Cost amounts
- Savings or Set Aside amounts
- Expense descriptions
- merchant information
- Budget balances
- Budget allocations
- transaction identifiers
- Budget or Plan identifiers
- Fixed Cost categories
- recurring financial descriptions
- expense attachment names
- expense attachment contents
- arbitrary user-entered text
- Apple ID
- Budgets account ID
- Supabase authentication user ID
- email address
- authentication tokens
- banking credentials
- bank-account information

Analytics are not used for advertising.

Analytics are not used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

---

## 4. Analytics Consent and Opt-Out

Anonymous analytics are collected only after you explicitly opt in.

You can change your choice at any time in:

**Budgets → Settings → Share Anonymous Analytics**

If you disable analytics:

- Budgets stops recording new analytics events
- Budgets stops uploading analytics events
- unsent analytics events stored locally are deleted
- your financial information is not affected
- Cloud Sync is not affected
- your Budgets account is not affected

If you later enable analytics again, collection begins prospectively.

Budgets does not reconstruct or upload activity that occurred while analytics were disabled.

Analytics that were already transmitted before you disabled analytics remain subject to the retention policy described below.

---

## 5. Analytics Retention

Anonymous usage analytics are retained for no more than **90 days** under Budgets' current retention policy.

Budgets automatically removes analytics records older than 90 days.

This retention process applies only to anonymous analytics.

It does not delete your financial information, synchronized account data, Expenses, Recurring information, Budgets, Plans, or attachments.

---

## 6. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Budgets is used only as necessary to:

- provide budgeting and financial-planning features
- store and display your financial information
- calculate Budget allocations and remaining amounts
- calculate Spending Pace
- calculate Available for Budgets and Unallocated amounts
- process Recurring Income and Fixed Costs
- maintain Budget Period and rollover calculations
- synchronize data between authorized devices
- restore synchronized data to another or reinstalled device
- maintain the correctness and reliability of financial history
- process expense attachments
- authenticate your account
- diagnose synchronization and application reliability issues
- understand anonymous feature usage when you have opted into analytics
- improve Budgets

Budgets does not sell your personal information.

Budgets does not use your information for targeted advertising.

Budgets does not use your information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

---

## 7. Expense Attachments

Budgets may allow you to attach images or other supported files to Expenses.

If you use Budgets locally without Cloud Sync, attachments are stored as part of the app's local data.

If you use Cloud Sync, attachment files and related metadata may be transmitted to and stored by Budgets' cloud infrastructure so that they can be accessed from authorized synchronized devices.

Attachment contents are treated as financial data.

Attachment contents are not included in anonymous usage analytics.

---

## 8. Data Export

Budgets provides an **Export Data** feature that allows you to export portions of your financial information.

Depending on the information you have created, exports may include source records such as:

- Budgets
- Plans and Plan Budgets
- Expenses
- Transfers
- Recurring Income
- Fixed Costs
- Savings or Set Aside records
- amounts
- frequencies or cadences
- applicable categories or types

Exported files may contain sensitive financial information.

You are responsible for protecting exported files after they leave Budgets.

An exported file should not necessarily be considered a complete technical backup of every internal synchronization record, attachment, revision, or historical state used by the application.

## 9. Service Providers

Budgets relies on third-party infrastructure to provide certain functionality.

### Apple

Apple provides services including:

- App Store and TestFlight distribution
- Sign in with Apple
- operating-system services used by Budgets

Your use of Apple services is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

### Supabase

Budgets uses Supabase infrastructure to provide services including:

- authenticated Cloud Sync
- database storage
- private expense attachment storage
- synchronization infrastructure
- anonymous usage analytics

Financial information stored through Cloud Sync is protected using account-based authorization rules designed to prevent one Budgets account from accessing another account's financial data.

Anonymous analytics are maintained separately from the financial synchronization system.

As with other internet services, infrastructure providers may process technical network information necessary to provide, secure, and operate their services.

---

## 10. Data Security

Budgets uses technical safeguards intended to protect synchronized information.

These safeguards include:

- authenticated access controls
- database row-level security
- account ownership checks
- private attachment storage
- transactional synchronization operations
- protections against stale or conflicting device data
- synchronization revision and dataset protections
- restricted analytics ingestion
- separation between financial information and anonymous analytics

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Budgets is designed to minimize unnecessary collection, limit access to financial information, and keep anonymous analytics separate from account-linked financial records.

---

## 11. Data Deletion

### Local Data

If you use Budgets without Cloud Sync, your financial information resides on your device.

Deleting Budgets from your device removes locally stored app data from that installation unless the information is otherwise preserved through an operating-system or device backup.

### Synchronized Financial Data

Budgets provides controls for deleting synchronized financial information.

Deleting synchronized financial data may remove that information from your Budgets cloud account and authorized synchronized devices.

This may include synchronized:

- Budgets
- Plans and Plan Budgets
- Expenses
- Transfers
- Recurring Income
- Fixed Costs
- Savings or Set Aside records
- financial history
- attachment information

### Account Deletion

Where available in the app, you may request deletion of your Budgets account and associated cloud information using Budgets' account-management controls.

Deleting an account may remove synchronized financial information associated with that account.

For additional privacy or deletion questions, contact the developer using the information below.

---

## 12. Data Portability

Budgets provides tools that may allow you to export portions of your financial information for your own use.

The exported information is intended to make your data more accessible outside the app.

Because Budgets maintains internal synchronization, revision, attachment, and historical state required to operate correctly, an export should not necessarily be considered a complete restorable backup of the application's internal database.

## Summary

Budgets is designed around the following privacy principles:

- You can use Budgets without creating an account.
- Your financial data can remain entirely local if you do not use Cloud Sync.
- Budgets does not require access to your bank account.
- Budgets does not require banking credentials.
- Cloud Sync is optional and is used only when you choose to sign in.
- Recurring Income, Fixed Costs, Savings, Budgets, Plans, Expenses, Transfers, and related financial information are treated as financial data.
- Anonymous analytics are optional and require explicit consent.
- Anonymous analytics are kept separate from your financial records.
- Anonymous analytics are not linked to your Budgets account.
- Anonymous analytics do not contain your financial amounts, Budget names, Recurring financial records, Expense descriptions, or other financial content.
- Anonymous analytics are retained for no more than 90 days.
- Budgets does not sell your data.
- Budgets does not use your data for targeted advertising.
- Budgets does not use your data for cross-app or cross-website tracking.
